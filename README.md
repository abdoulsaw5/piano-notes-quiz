# Piano Notes Quiz

An interactive web-based game to help you learn how to read piano sheet music. Built for beginner pianists who want to practice identifying notes on the staff.


## Why I built this

I was learning piano and looking for something more hands on. I learn better by quizzing and challenging myself rather than just reading so instead of staring at a chart of notes, I built this so I could actually practice them interactively. It helped me so much that I decided to turn it into a project others could use too.

## What it does

- Shows a note on a musical staff and asks you to identify it
- Three modes: Right Hand (treble clef), Left Hand (bass clef), and Both Hands mixed
- Multiple choice answers pick from A, B, C, D, E, F, G
- Tracks how many you got wrong out of 10 questions
- Shows which notes you need to review at the end

## How to play

1. Choose a mode on the home screen
2. Look at the note displayed on the staff
3. Click the correct note name from the 4 choices
4. Get through all 10 questions and see your score

## Notes covered

- **Treble clef lines:** E G B D F  *"Every Good Boy Does Fine"*
- **Treble clef spaces:** F A C E  *"FACE"*
- **Bass clef lines:** G B D F A  *"Good Boys Do Fine Always"*
- **Bass clef spaces:** A C E G  *"All Cows Eat Grass"*
